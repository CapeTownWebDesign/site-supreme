## Hair services in Somerset West

Book one service or combine them into a single visit. Every plan is built around your hair, not a fixed menu.

[Book a consultation](#visit)

- ### Cut and finish

  A cut shaped around how your hair grows and how you wear it, finished with a blow-dry so you see the result before you leave.
- ### Colour

  Root touch-ups, all-over colour, highlights and balayage. Every plan starts from the level your hair is now and the level you want.
- ### Blow-dry and styling

  Smooth, bouncy or sleek, for an ordinary week or a particular evening.
- ### Treatments

  Conditioning and repair treatments matched to what your hair needs after sun, salt, chlorine, heat or colour.
- ### Bridal and occasion hair

  A trial first, then the day itself. Styles planned around the dress, the veil and the Cape wind.

## Colour is measured in levels.

Hair colour sits on a scale from 1, the darkest black, to 10, the lightest blonde. A colourist starts at the level your hair is now, agrees the level you want, and plans the steps between.

A move of more than a few levels is often spread over several visits, which keeps your hair in good condition along the way.
