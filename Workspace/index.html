Common questions

Before you ask.

What does a project actually cost?

It depends on scope — a landing page costs far less than a booking system with a database behind it. You'll always get a clear quote before any work starts, no surprise line items after.

How long does a website take to build?

Most business websites launch in 2–4 weeks from our first call. More complex builds — booking systems, portals — take longer, and we'll give you a real timeline once we know the scope.

Do I own the website once it's built?

Yes. The site, the code, and your content are yours. Our care plans cover hosting and maintenance, but you're never locked in.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small edits are covered under a care plan. Bigger additions — a new page, a booking feature — are scoped and quoted like any new project.

Do you handle hosting too?

Yes — hosting, security updates, and uptime monitoring are part of every care plan, so you're not left managing that yourself.
